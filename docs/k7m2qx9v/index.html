The five questions

plain language

Can we get out of it?

Yes, but not yet. After the first 12 months you can terminate for convenience on 90 days' written notice. For breach, 30 days to remedy, then immediate termination. Harbour must provide 60 days of exit assistance at the contract rates.

cl. 14.2, 14.4, 19.1

What does it cost us?

₹1.24 crore a year, invoiced monthly in arrears, payable in 45 days. Rates rise annually by CPI capped at +5%. You have committed to 80% of forecast volume — shortfall is billed anyway.

cl. 7.1, 7.3, sch. 2

What do we have to do, and by when?

Submit a rolling volume forecast by the 5th of each month, name a contract manager, give access to the Bhiwandi and Hosur sites, and respond to service failure notices within 5 business days.

cl. 5.2, 6.1, sch. 3

What happens if they fail?

Service credits up to 10% of the monthly charge — and credits are your sole remedy for delay. Overall liability is capped at 12 months' fees, but the cap does not apply to data breach, IP infringement or fraud.

cl. 11.2, 12.1, 12.3

Who can change it?

Only a written change order signed by both contract managers. Email agreement is expressly excluded, so a varied rate agreed in a thread is not binding.

cl. 20.4
