Commonly asked questions

plain language

Can we get out of it?

Not before 30 June 2028 — there is no termination for convenience during the initial term. You may terminate for a material breach Lumen fails to remedy within 30 days, or immediately on their insolvency. After the initial term, 60 days' notice before any renewal date.

cl. 12.1, 12.3, 3.2

What does it cost us?

₹86,40,000 a year for 1,200 named users, invoiced annually in advance and payable within 30 days. Additional users are trued up quarterly at the same per-user rate. At renewal the fee may rise by no more than 7%.

cl. 6.1, 6.3, 6.5

What do we have to do, and by when?

Name an administrator, keep the named-user list accurate, ensure your people follow the acceptable use policy — you are responsible for what your users do — and pay in advance. Lumen may suspend access if an undisputed invoice is more than 30 days late.

cl. 4.2, 4.4, 6.6

What happens if they fail?

99.5% monthly uptime. Below that you earn service credits up to 15% of the monthly fee, and credits are your sole remedy for downtime. Liability is capped at 12 months' fees, with no cap for data breach, breach of confidence or IP infringement.

cl. 8.2, 11.1, 11.3

Can they change the deal?

Yes, within limits. Lumen may update the service provided it does not materially degrade it, and may change the standard terms on 30 days' notice. If a change materially harms you, you have 30 days to terminate without penalty — but silence counts as acceptance. Commercial terms change only by a signed order form.

cl. 2.4, 17.2, 17.3
